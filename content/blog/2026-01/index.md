<span style="background-color: lightyellow;"><span style="font-size:22px">**(a) Microbiome and population health**</span></span>

{{< paper 
title="Baby-to-baby strain transmission shapes the developing gut microbiome"
url="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09983-z" 
journal="Nature, Jan 2026">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Very impactful study of dense strain-resolved shotgun metagenomics in a nursery cohort (43 babies; 1,013 stools; 3 sites). Authors show rapid baby-baby gut strain transmission (within ~3 months). Regarding origin of strains, nursery strains contribute as much or more than family strains; siblings can buffer acquisition and antibiotics amplify strain turnover/influx.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Human and bacterial genetic variation shape oral microbiomes and health"
url="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10037-7" 
journal="Nature, Jan 2026">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Re-analysis of saliva WGS from 12,519 people (SPARK) turns “human-genome” sequencing into the largest oral microbiome GWAS yet: 11 host loci (esp. FUT2 and AMY1 CNV) shape oral community composition and even bacterial genome content. Signals colocalize with dentures/tooth-loss risk in UK Biobank (AMY1/FUT2/PITX1), suggesting specific host–microbe genetic interactions in cariogenesis.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Methanogenic archaea bolster mucosal homeostasis and protect from colitis"
url="https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.01.19.700430v1" 
journal="bioRxiv (preprint), Jan 2026">}}
<b>Comment:</b> I love these studies starting to link the human archaeome with health outcomes (positive or not). Here, gnotobiotic/Abx mice colonized with human gut methanogens (M. smithii or M. stadtmanae) show thicker colonic mucus, more goblet cells, more Foxp3+ Tregs, and protection from DSS colitis vs controls. Mechanistically, M. smithii seems to retains partial benefit in Rag2-/- mice, while M. stadtmanae needs adaptive immunity, which leads to the author’s argument that archaea can be causal mucosal protectors (cautious but plausible!)
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Assembly of the infant gut microbiome and resistome are linked to bacterial strains in mother’s milk"
url="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-66497-y" 
journal="Nature Communications, November 2025">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Microbiota from 195 healthy breastfeeding mother-infant pairs identifies shared strains and ARGs between mother’s milk and infant stool, involving commensals (B. longum, B. bifidum) but also pathobionts like Klebsiella.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Assessing gut microbial provisioning of essential amino acids to host in a mouse model with reconstituted gut microbiomes"
url="https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-08966-0" 
journal="Communications Biology, Nov 2025">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Gut microbes can synthesize essential amino acids (EAAs), but how much these actually contribute to host EAA pools is unclear. Here, authors find no detectable microbial EAA across all organs examined, after analysing marked δ¹³C-EAA given to GF/conventionalized mice.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Harnessing the microbiome for cancer therapy"
url="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41579-025-01268-6" 
journal="Nature Reviews Microbiology, Jan 2026">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Interesting review on microbiome & cancer risk + treatment response. Addressing multiple topics in there, with also an interesting part on microbiome disruption being linked to ↓ drug efficacy across multiple cancer therapies (chemo/immuno) + tumour types.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Time-specific bidirectional links between the maternal microbiome, milk composition, and infant gut microbiota"
url="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1931312825004743?dgcid=rss_sd_all" 
journal="Cell Host and Microbe, Jan 2026">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Interesting! 152 mother-infant dyads from rural Burkina Faso; MISAME-III subset) with longitudinal metagenomics+milk 16S+HMOs/nutrients/metabolomics. Infant gut <2mo = 3 clusters (“Escherichia”/”Bifidobacterium”/diverse+pathogen-prevalent) + links with milk HMOs.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Studying low-dose antibiotic effects on the gut microbiome"
url="https://www.cell.com/trends/microbiology/fulltext/S0966-842X%2825%2900361-0" 
journal="Trends in Microbiology, Dec 2025">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Interesting review on sub-MIC antibiotic exposures from food/environment on the gut microbiome/resistome with suggested mechanisms impacting it. Interesting comments on methods/approaches + current regulatory framework to .
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Infant gut anellovirus composition associates with early life factors and childhood atopic disease"
url="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-66732-6" 
journal="Nature Communications, Dec 2025">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Anelloviruses are considered to be “commensal” viruses with no obvious disease links. Here, authors describe an ubiquitous & diverse anellovirome in infants with FUT2 secretors having ↑ viral abundance but no associations w/ atopic disease (minor w/ allergic rhinitis).
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Gut microbial ethanol metabolism contributes to auto-brewery syndrome in an observational cohort"
url="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41564-025-02225-y" 
journal="Nature Microbiology, Jan 2026">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Autobrewery syndrome is a rare disorder of alcohol intoxication due to gut microbial ethanol production. Using an observational ABS cohort (n=22), authors suggest mechanism/therapy signals, with an FMT attempt on 1 patient showing symptom improvement.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Evaluation of bacteriophages as a signature of microbiome health: a systematic review and meta-analysis"
url="https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanmic/article/PIIS2666-5247%2825%2900124-7/fulltext" 
journal="The Lancet Microbe, Dec 2025">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Interesting new meta-analysis in the Lancet Microbe, outlining phages as signatures or drivers of microbiome dysbiosis.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Identification of antimicrobial peptides from ancient gut microbiomes"
url="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-68495-0" 
journal="Nature Comms, Jan 2026">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Cool work! Mining 7 ancient (1-2ky) human coprolite metagenomes for ORFs with AMP potential: 160 peptide candidates > 40 synthesised > 36 active at <100 µM in vitro with ~2/3 being Segatella copri–derived.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Prospective association between the gut microbiota and incident pneumonia: a cohort study of 6419 individuals"
url="https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12931-025-03453-w" 
journal="Respiratory Research, Dec 2025">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Nice work from Finnish colleagues, looking at gut microbial associations from 685 pneumonia cases in 17.8y of FINRISK registry follow-up. Some limitations but butyrate-producers seem to associate with lower pneumonia risk & Clostridium_AQ innocuum with higher risk.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Unraveling the microbial landscape in prostate cancer: pathogenesis to therapy"
url="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41522-025-00900-w" 
journal="npj Biofilms and Microbiomes, Jan 2026">}}
<b>Comment:</b> New review on role of microbiota in prostate cancer, expliciting the “gut–prostate axis”. Interesting examples of FMT/commensals modulating ADT response (therapy used to slow PC growth). Good caution+criticism on studies w/ potential caveats on low biomass/risk of FP.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="The human gut microbiome in enteric infections: from association to translation"
url="https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/19490976.2026.2612836" 
journal="Gut Microbes, Jan 2026">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Nice review from Alex Almeida’s group on gut microbiome & enteric infection susceptibility/severity, including focus on some recurring susceptibility associations: decrease of α-diversity+butyrate producers & Proteobacteria/Enterobacteriaceae blooms.
{{< /paper >}}

<span style="background-color: lightyellow;"><span style="font-size:22px">**(b) Microbial ecology and genomics, AMR, etc.**</span></span>

{{< paper 
title="Real-time simultaneous monitoring of multiple analytes in bacterial cultures"
url="https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/aem.01810-25" 
journal="AEM, Nov 2025">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Pretty cool! Engineered periplasmic binding proteins+fluorophore sensors (for sugars+AAs) are used to report real-time extracellular metabolite dynamics in E. coli + many others species after 24h in plate readers, showing hierarchical substrate use.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Airborne Bacterial Communities: Diversity, Survival Strategies and Functional Roles in the Atmosphere"
url="https://enviromicro-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/1758-2229.70274" 
journal="Environmental Microbiology Reports, Jan 2026">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Reading about bacteria in the atmospheric water cycle is always fun. In this review, authors think about the possible selective pressures on bacteria in the atmosphere after aerosolization.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Ecological ubiquity and phylogeny drive nestedness in phages–bacteria networks and shape the bacterial defensome"
url="https://journals.plos.org/plospathogens/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.ppat.1013428" 
journal="Plos Pathogens, Dec 2025">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Interesting phage host-range network between 23 apricot-orchard phages & 44 P. syringae strains w/ varying disease outcome. Findings include strong nestedness for phage susceptibility (i.e bacterial phylogroup>ecological origin) + interesting genomic links.
{{< /paper >}}

<span style="background-color: lightyellow;"><span style="font-size:22px">**(c) Other general interests**</span></span>

{{< paper 
title="Preventive interventions for vasovagal reactions in whole blood donors: a cluster-randomised, stepped-wedge, crossover trial of 73 sites involving 1·4 million donors in England"
url="https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanhae/article/PIIS2352-3026%2825%2900319-9/fulltext" 
journal="The Lancet Haematology, Jan 2026">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Very large RCT (STRIDES Trial, 73 UK blood donation sites; 1.3M donors; >4M donations) shows no meaningful impact of additional strategies to prevent vasovagal syncope during whole-blood donation. A good way to assess whether “standard practice” actually works!
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Towards the endgame: achieving elimination of viral hepatitis in England"
url="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666524725002605?dgcid=rss_sd_all"
journal="The Lancet Microbe, Jan 2026">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Interesting commentary on the elimiation of viral hepatitis in England: HBV/HCV mortality targets exceeded, HBV vertical transmission elimination achieved. However still many chronic HBV cases, no national registry & disinvestment in harm-reduction approaches.
{{< /paper >}}

{{< paper 
title="Minimum combined sleep, physical activity, and nutrition variations associated with lifeSPAN and healthSPAN improvements: a population cohort study"
url="https://www.thelancet.com/journals/eclinm/article/PIIS2589-5370%2825%2900676-5/fulltext" 
journal="eClinicalMedicine, Jan 2026">}}
<b>Comment:</b> Interesting new study analysing data from wearables from subset of UKBiobank participants. Optimal combo (+9.45y lifespan vs worst tertiles) was sleep 7.2–8.0 h/d, min exercise >42 min/d, good diet quality score. Minimal changes “added” several years of life.
{{< /paper >}}
